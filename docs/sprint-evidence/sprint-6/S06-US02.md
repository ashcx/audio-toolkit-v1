# S06-US02 honest sample-peak normalization

Normalization analyzes and replays an owned OPFS float-PCM checkpoint at its actual recipe position. It never re-decodes the input or creates an intermediate compressed file. Trim-before-normalize and normalize-before-trim produce different expected measured peaks. Repeated normalization, mono/stereo silence and temporary-file cleanup are exercised through downloaded WAVs.

Independent review reproduced extreme amplification of near-silence (1e-11 → 0.891 at -1 dBFS, approximately +219 dB). Parent reran that exact reproduction. Resolved contract retains peaks at/below -120 dBFS unchanged, caps positive normalization gain at +60 dB and labels prevented targets `Normalization limited.` Unit checks cover 1e-11 and 1e-4; the browser suite exports a quantized quiet source and verifies the cap and label. Non-silent normal targets remain within 0.01 dB subject to PCM16 precision. LUFS/true-peak processing remains excluded.

Three-second and 180-second synthetic recordings exercise normalization. Both must report one decode, one terminal encoder, one normalization checkpoint and blocks at most 4096 frames; only final OPFS output remains, and removal empties job storage. This proves the implemented checkpoint/block behavior over these fixtures, not complete process-memory scaling. Full workload/RSS limits remain Sprint 11. Final exact results are in coverage.md and dsp/results.json.
