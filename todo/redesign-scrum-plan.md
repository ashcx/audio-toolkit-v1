# Audio Toolkit Scrum delivery policy

## Read the source of truth

The feature plan owns scope. TODO.md owns summary status. Orchestration owns dependencies and freezes. Sprint checklists own execution acceptance. Preserve historical brief text. The conventional filename here plans a new app, not an existing redesign.

## Plan verifiable increments

Use one-week timeboxes, 6 committed SP plus 3 required stretch SP. Re-estimate committed work to at most 7 SP with 20–30% review/rework reserve. Points are not hours. These are initial estimates; split oversized increments into numbered fractional sprints before execution without dropping acceptance. Every increment owns one PR.

Each sprint follows Goal, Entry criteria, Depends on, Tasks, Exit criteria. Stories have IDs, priority, points, main-instance owner, Given/When/Then and named evidence. Verify Ready, ground affected sources, freeze contracts, capture baseline, implement minimally, drive the actual product, self-review and rerun, then log before advancing.

Evidence belongs under `docs/sprint-evidence/sprint-N/`. `coverage.md` maps every Ready/Done condition and story to an exact command, observation or artifact using VERIFIED, NOT VERIFIED or INCONCLUSIVE. Keep exact results, lawful fixture metadata, browser/device versions, audio measurements and applicable desktop/mobile captures. Baseline failures are separate from regressions. Deterministic checks stay offline. Protect private files and filenames.

The append-only show-me-your-work trail is `docs/sprint-evidence/decisions.tsv`, not another task list. Record actual agent-run planning, written daily sync, review and retrospective events. At expiry record unfinished work and a recovery timebox. Do not invent meetings or user sessions.

## Integrate pstack deliberately

Read installed poteto-mode, Codex mapping and matching leaf instructions before use. This planning run applies multi-phase-plan intent with scrum-mode's schema, paths and acceptance. Its generic skeleton/validator, separate program store, mandatory prototypes, ten-worker fan-out, automation and human approval gates do not apply. Empirical probes are Sprint 1 work and remain NOT VERIFIED now. No product implementation is authorized by planning.

Route grounding to how, material contract choices to architect, cheap clear regression targets to tdd, measurements to benchmark-checklist, decision trails to show-me-your-work, prose to technical-writing/unslop and sprint review to interrogate. Read each leaf before applying. Do not modify installed skills or global configuration.

## Require independent interrogate

After implementation, actual-product checks and separate self-review, review the whole integrated sprint and acceptance matrix. Default reviewer is gpt-6.1-terra at high. Add at most one gpt-6-luna at xhigh only when another perspective adds value, following the user's overriding review preference. No Astra or extra synthesizer. Reviewers operate read-only or in isolated scratch/worktrees and never implement changes.

Give reviewers intent, base/head and working snapshot, requirements, every Ready/Done row, contract freezes, captures, exact checks, decisions and unfinished-work claims. Combine correctness, safety, user journey, coverage and maintainability lenses. Behavior findings require actual reproduction; process findings require checks against actual repository records. The parent reruns allegations, drops unsupported claims, fixes sequentially and obtains fresh affected review. Missing independent review stays NOT VERIFIED. Self-review is not a substitute. Save scope, models, coverage, findings, dropped claims, fixes/reruns and verdict in `interrogate.md`.

## Deliver and progress autonomously after implementation authorization

No routine human acceptance or next-sprint checkbox applies. Complete requires all stories including stretch, verified exits, independent review and PR delivery. Deliver the one PR after technical/review gates; include plans, exact checks, captures, gaps and dependency revisions. Record verified URL in sprint References/log and roadmap, push bookkeeping and observe CI state. Merge and deployment follow actual authorization.

Last-resort progression requires exhausted distinct viable fixes, actual functional evidence, no safety/correctness/shared-contract compromise and proven downstream independence. Use scrum-mode's unfinished template at `todo/sprint-N-unfinished.md`. Interrogate must verify the exception. Leave unfinished boxes unchecked and sprint In progress. Missing review cannot qualify. Recheck independence at each subsequent entry; full completion requires all required outcomes or explicit user rescoping.
